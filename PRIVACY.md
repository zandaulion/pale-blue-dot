# Privacy Policy — Pale Blue Dot

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

Pale Blue Dot ("the app") is an interactive 3D globe and astronomy app. This
policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

The app has **no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking**,
and it does **not** send your personal data to us or anyone else. We (the
developer) do not operate a server and do not collect personal data about you.

## Information the app accesses

The app requests **no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or personal
information**. Its only permission is internet access.

**Internet access.**
The app downloads public, openly available data to display on the globe:

- Cloud / satellite imagery from NASA
- Recent earthquake data from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)
- Volcano, wildfire, and severe-storm reports from NASA EONET

These are standard requests to public services. The app does **not** include any
personal information, location, or identifier in these requests. As with any
internet request, the third-party service you connect to (e.g., NASA, USGS) may
see your device's IP address as a normal part of delivering the data; their
handling of that is governed by their own privacy policies.

**On-device choices.** Named places, their coordinates and optional time zones,
widget selections, saved event observations, journey/notebook progress, and
preferences stay in app-private storage on the device. The app does not request
device location to create a place; you choose one from a catalog or tap the globe.
The live wallpaper and widget use the same public-data cache and do not send
these saved choices to the developer.

**Language and audio preferences.** Your chosen app language, first-launch
read-aloud choice, and music settings are remembered locally. Android's system
app-language settings also manage the locale on supported versions.

## Read-aloud audio

First launch offers a choice to explore with Talia or quietly. You can change
Read aloud in Settings. Talia's fixed-card recordings are generated by the
developer using ElevenLabs and bundled with the app in all 12 supported
languages. Playback works offline and makes no requests to ElevenLabs. The app
contains no ElevenLabs API key and does not record your voice.

Changing event facts and challenge scores use your device's text-to-speech
engine; it is also used when a recording cannot play and a suitable voice is
available. The app passes the displayed narration text to that engine. Whether
speech runs entirely on-device depends on your selected engine and installed
voices; a network voice may send text to its provider under that provider's
privacy policy. The app does not send saved place names or notebook records to
ElevenLabs. The wallpaper and widget do not play narration or music.

## Sharing images

The app includes a **Share** feature that lets you capture an image of your
current view of the globe and share it using your device's standard share
options (for example, to a messaging or social app of your choice).

- Images are created **on your device** and are only shared when **you** choose
  to share them. Sharing is placed behind a parental gate.
- Once you share an image to another app, **that app's privacy policy** governs
  what happens to it.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not have user accounts or require sign-in.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
- We do not operate a server that receives your data.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.
