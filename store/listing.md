## en-US

### Name

Pale Blue Dot

### Short description

Explore a living 3D Earth, learn its science, and bring it to your home screen

### Full description

Pale Blue Dot is a calm, interactive Earth and sky for curious children, teens and grown-ups. Spin the globe to see sunlight move across our planet and city lights appear at night.

EXPLORE EARTH
• See day and night in real time, animated auroras, and clouds from recent NASA imagery or an offline generated view.
• Explore recently reported earthquakes, volcanoes, wildfires and storms. Event cards show their date and source; current reports need an internet connection.
• Show tectonic plate boundaries and optional equator, tropic and polar-circle guides. Tap a place to learn about its daylight. Touch and hold to revisit day, night and sky lessons after their first appearance.

TRAVEL THROUGH TIME AND SPACE
• Move the clock up to 24 hours forward or back, or choose a date within a year to compare the seasons. Return to now with one tap.
• Look beyond Earth at stars, the Milky Way, 15 constellations, the Sun and Moon. A thin outline and label help you find an unlit new Moon or small crescent.
• Explore an illustrated ISS orbit and approximate Sun–Moon alignment alerts.

LEARN YOUR WAY
• Follow guided journeys about sunrise, the Ring of Fire and the seasons. Collect discoveries in a Field notebook and try find-it challenges.
• Check the Today briefing for the Moon phase, daylight at a saved place and a recent Earth observation.
• At first launch, choose whether Talia reads prepared lessons aloud. Her recordings work offline in all 12 app languages. A separate Android voice option reads changing facts and scores. Music has its own volume control.
• Choose a language in Settings or follow your device: English, German, Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Romanian, Ukrainian or Simplified Chinese.

MAKE EARTH YOURS
• Save places from an offline catalog of over 300 cities or pick a point on the globe; see local time, sunrise and sunset.
• Add the Earth Today widget to your home screen, or choose one of three live Earth wallpapers.

No ads, purchases, accounts or tracking. The app does not request your location; saved places stay on your device. Sharing is behind a parental gate.

Earth imagery and data: NASA and USGS. City data: GeoNames contributors (CC BY 4.0). Plate boundaries: Bird (2003), PB2002.

## ja-JP

### Name

### Short description

### Full description

Pale Blue Dotは、好奇心いっぱいの子どもから大人まで、地球と空をゆったり探検できるアプリです。地球儀を回すと、太陽の光が移動し、夜には街の明かりがともる様子が見られます。

地球を探検
• 昼と夜をリアルタイムで観察。動くオーロラや、NASAの最近の画像に基づく雲、オフラインで生成した雲も表示できます。
• 最近報告された地震、火山、山火事、嵐を調べられます。カードには日付と出典を表示。最新の報告を取得するにはインターネットが必要です。
• プレート境界や、赤道・回帰線・極圏のガイドを表示できます。場所をタップして昼の長さを学ぼう。昼・夜・空の解説は最初の発見後、長押しで再表示できます。

時間と宇宙を旅する
• 時刻を前後24時間まで動かしたり、1年以内の日付を選んで季節を比べたりできます。
• 星、天の川、15の星座、太陽と月を眺めよう。細い輪郭線とラベルが、光っていない新月や細い月の位置を示します。
• 国際宇宙ステーションの模式的な軌道や、太陽と月の位置関係に基づく目安の通知もあります。

自分のペースで学ぶ
• 日の出、環太平洋火山帯、季節についてのガイド付きの旅へ。発見をフィールドノートに集め、探すチャレンジにも挑戦できます。
